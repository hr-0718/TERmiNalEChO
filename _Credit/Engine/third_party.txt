Third-Party Libraries and Assets Distributed with the Web Release Game
====================================================================

Target: Web / WebGPU
Complete license texts are in third_party/. Game-owned notices are in ../Game/.
The selected component's bundled notices are retained as a complete collection.

Common engine components:
Effekseer HEFK runtime and WebGPU renderer - MIT - https://github.com/effekseer/Effekseer
LLGI WebGPU backend - zlib license - https://github.com/altseed/LLGI
Maximilian - MIT and bundled permissive notices - https://github.com/micknoise/maximilian
miniaudio - MIT-0 alternative - https://github.com/mackron/miniaudio
pl_mpeg video decoder - MIT - https://github.com/phoboslab/pl_mpeg
RapidJSON - MIT and upstream bundled notices - https://github.com/Tencent/rapidjson
simdjson 4.0.7 - MIT, Boost, BSD and bundled source notices - https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson
Noto Sans JP - SIL Open Font License 1.1 - https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+JP

Web runtime components:
DirectXMath scalar headers, SAL annotations, Emscripten and its bundled system
runtime, Emdawnwebgpu, Dawn and WebGPU headers. Source/version details are in
third_party/NOTICE_WebRuntime.txt. SDK tools are not shipped with the game.
